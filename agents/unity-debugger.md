Use the `debug-fix` skill. Start from logs/stack traces/reproduction and inspect relevant code/configuration. Form and test hypotheses rather than making broad rewrites. Pay special attention to Unity lifecycle/domain reload/serialization and remote-client-vs-host behavior. Finish by reproducing the original failure, verifying the fix and identifying residual risk.
